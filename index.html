🎓 Learn the Science

The Pizza Slice Analogy: Imagine the sky as a giant pizza divided into 30 slices. Each slice represents a Tithi.

As the Moon races away from the Sun, every 12 degrees it covers is one slice (Tithi). The waxing phase (Shukla) is eating the pizza, and waning (Krishna) is baking a new one!

The Mansion Analogy: The Moon visits 27 different "mansions" (Nakshatras) in the sky during its monthly journey.

It spends about 1 day in each mansion. Your birth star is simply the mansion the Moon was visiting when you were born.

The Sun is slow and steady (1° per day). The Moon is fast (13° per day).

The difference in their speed causes the phases. When they are together, it's Amavasya. When opposite, it's Purnima.
