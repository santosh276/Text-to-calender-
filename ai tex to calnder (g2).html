Calendar Markup Language

Create a rich, detailed calendar month using a dedicated block. All event text supports standard formatting like **bold** and bilingual syntax `[like this]`.

::: calendar
++ September 2025 ++
Description: Odia Kohinoor Calendar [ଓଡ଼ିଆ କୋହିନୂର କ୍ୟାଲେଣ୍ଡର]
Full Moon: 9
New Moon: 24

4 ! Nuakhai [ନୂଆଖାଇ]
8 ! Radha Ashtami [ରାଧା ଅଷ୍ଟମୀ]
11 ! Sunia [ସୁନିଆ]
14 ! Anant Chaturdashi [ଅନନ୍ତ ଚତୁର୍ଦଶୀ]
30 ! Durga Puja Begins [ଦୁର୍ଗା ପୂଜା ଆରମ୍ଭ]

25 > Pay electricity bill (Finance)
   @ Due Date: 28th

::: notes
++ Month Highlights [ମାସର ମୁଖ୍ୟାଂଶ] ++
- Durga Puja begins at the end of the month.
- Nuakhai is a major agricultural festival.
:::
:::
Structure
    ::: calendar
  • Start and end the block with ::: calendar and :::.
    • ++ ... ++
  • Define the month and year.
    • Key: Val
  • Set month-wide data like Full Moon: 9 or Description: ....
    • ::: notes
  • Create a notes section for the sidebar.
Day Entry Syntax

Use a prefix to define the type of information for a specific day. Indent lines to add more details to the same day.

    Day ! Event
  • A Holiday or major public event.
    • Day > Event
  • A general event or appointment.
    • Day * Event
  • A special occasion like a birthday or anniversary.
    • Day ~ Text
  • A Tithi or other lunar/astrological note.
    • Day @ Key: Val
  • A Panchang detail (e.g., @ Sunrise: 06:10).
    • (Tag)
  • Add a tag like (Work) or (Health) to color-code events.
